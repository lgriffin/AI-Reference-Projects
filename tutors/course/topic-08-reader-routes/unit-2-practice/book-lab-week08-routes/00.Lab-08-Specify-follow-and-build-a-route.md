# Objectives

Start rung 5 from rung 4's standard version. Specify a new route for the reader before any code exists and
watch it fail three ways. Then read the contract, follow one URL through the reader from its folder to the
card you click, follow another to its 404, and build your route by imitating what you traced. Break three
rules, find the same flow in tutors-mono-repo, build beyond, and end with a Kaizen step.

## Learning outcomes

| Outcome | Level | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain why `tutors.json` is the contract, and what the reader adds to it | Understand | 4 |
| Specify a reader route first, see it fail, then build the route, its load and its logic | Apply | 3, 7 |
| Trace a URL from its route folder to the component, here and upstream | Analyse | 5, 6, 9 |
| Diagnose a Loader, Framework or Rung Fence failure from its message | Analyse | 8 |
| Judge whether a feature is pulled or pushed, and whether a scenario or a browser should prove it | Evaluate | 3, 7, 11 |
| Design an extension that starts from a student's need | Create | 10 |

Each step's heading names its Bloom level. The outcomes serve module outcomes M1, M2, M3, M4 and M6.

You need Node.js 22.18 or later, pnpm 10, Git, a browser, and the `LADDER` and `MONO` variables from Lab 1
(`LADDER` is `AI-Reference-Projects/tutors`, `MONO` your clone of tutors-mono-repo). Step 9 runs the
upstream reader, which needs Node 26.13 or later; it also works by reading. Allow about two and a half
hours. Commit after each step that changes code, so that every break can be undone with `git checkout .`.
