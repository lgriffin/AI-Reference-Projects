## What this week adds

Rung 5 is a copy of rung 4 plus one app, `apps/reader`. This week is the reader's inside:

- **the contract**: the reader fetches `tutors.json` and never touches the generator;
- **routing**: a URL is a folder under `src/routes`, with `[courseid]` and `[...loid]` parameters;
- **load**: each route's `+page.ts` asks the course service for a page, or for a 404 message;
- **specified first**: nine new requirements, R-013 to R-021, written before the code they describe.

Next week is what a student sees: runes, components, the three UI layers and browser tests. It ends with
rung 5's assignment and its standard version.

## The value it delivers

Rung 5's customer is the **student reading a course**. In week 1 you mapped the Tutors value stream from a
lecturer's folder to a student's screen. Rung 5 is the last step of that stream: the moment a link becomes
a page. The value is the right page, without waiting, from any link, and a clear answer when a link is
wrong.
