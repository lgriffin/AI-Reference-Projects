## What the scenarios do not do

Now an experiment. Delete the unit test you wrote in step 5 ("a page's title runs from the page back to
the course") and remove `pageTitle` from that file's import. Then:

~~~bash
pnpm test
~~~

~~~
      Tests  433 passed | 6 skipped (439)
~~~

Green. The R-023 scenarios still pass, so the requirement is still proved. Now:

~~~bash
pnpm test:mutation
pnpm check:mutation-floors
~~~

~~~
      lo-tree.ts          |  95.08 |  100.00 |       58 |         0 |          0 |        3 |        0 |
...
below-floor: apps/reader/src/lib/course/lo-tree.ts 95.08% < floor 100%. A test stopped killing a mutant: open reports/mutation/index.html, find the survivors, and add the test that kills them.
~~~

Three mutants with **no coverage**, all in `pageTitle`, although two scenarios exercise it. Why? Read the
last section of `tests/TESTING.md`: a vitest-cucumber step is a test of its own, so Stryker's per-test
coverage cannot rerun a `Then` that depends on an earlier `Given`. The mutation run therefore leaves the
BDD steps out, and the unit tests in `tests/unit/reader` are what Stryker runs.

So the two tiers have two jobs:

| Tier | Its job |
| --- | --- |
| scenario | proves the **requirement**, in words a reader of the course can follow |
| unit test | pins the **code** closely enough that a changed line is noticed |

Restore the test (`git checkout tests/unit/reader/lo-tree.test.ts`) and confirm the floor holds again, or
simply trust the first run.
