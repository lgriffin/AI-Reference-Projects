# Objectives

Specify a small change to what a student sees before you build it. See runes at work and break one. Trace
a card through the three UI layers and break two layer rules. Make your specification green, then find out
why the browser still disagrees, and fix it. Write browser tests, judge the suite by mutation, find the
same ideas upstream, compare your rung 5 with the standard version, build beyond, and end with a Kaizen
step. The rung's assignment, a next and previous navigator, follows this lab.

## Learning outcomes

| Outcome | Level | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain `$props`, `$state`, `$derived` and a shared rune, and how the three UI layers depend | Understand | 3, 4 |
| Specify a user-interface behaviour first, move its rule into plain logic, and prove it by a scenario and in a browser | Apply | 2, 5, 6 |
| Diagnose which check catches a change, and explain a failure a scenario cannot see | Analyse | 3, 4, 5, 6 |
| Judge which tier should prove a rule, and judge the suite by mutation score against its floors | Evaluate | 6, 7, 8 |
| Compare your work with the standard version, and extend the variant | Evaluate, Create | 9, 10 |

Each step's heading names its Bloom level. The outcomes serve module outcomes M3, M4 and M6.

Work in the `tutors-ladder/rung-5-reader` folder from Lab 8, with your wall route (R-022) committed and
`../fixtures` beside it. Run `git status` (clean) and `pnpm test` (green: `429 passed | 6 skipped`) before
you start. If you did not finish Lab 8, do its steps 3 and 7 first: this lab builds on them. Start
`pnpm dev` in a terminal of its own. Allow about two and a half hours. Every break is undone with
`git checkout .`.
