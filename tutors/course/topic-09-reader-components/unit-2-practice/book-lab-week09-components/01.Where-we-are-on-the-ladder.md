## What this week adds

Last week stopped where a `load` returns its data. This week is everything after that:

- **runes**: `$props`, `$state`, `$derived`, and shared state behind `.value`;
- **components** in three layers, `ui-primitives`, `ui-navigators` and `ui-components`, mirrored from
  upstream's packages and kept by the Reader layering rule;
- **browser tests**: the built reader in Chromium, driven by the `playwright` library;
- **specifying an interface**: which part of a rule a scenario proves, and which part only a browser can.

This is rung 5's last week. It ends with the assignment (A5), the exit criteria on the topic page, and a
comparison with rung 5's standard version.

## The value it delivers

The customer is still the student reading a course. Last week you asked "who pulled this feature?". This
week you ask "did they get it?". The only place to check is the screen.
