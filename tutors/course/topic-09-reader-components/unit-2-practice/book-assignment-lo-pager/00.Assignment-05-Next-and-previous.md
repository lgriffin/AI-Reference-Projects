# Assignment 5: a next and previous navigator

Rung 5's coding assignment (A5 in the module descriptor), assessed at the **Create** level of Bloom's
taxonomy.

Add a navigator to the rung 5 reader that lets a student page through a course: from a talk, note or lab
to the previous and the next one, across units and topics, without going back to the topic page.

Upstream's lab has a step pager (`content/lab/Lab.svelte`), and its course navigation
(`packages/svelte/ui-navigators/src/CourseNavigation.svelte` with `LoContextTree.svelte`) shows a student
where they are in the course. You will build a pager between learning objects, in the navigators layer.

The code is short. The assignment is the whole of rung 5 in one change: the rule **specified first**, its
logic in plain TypeScript where a scenario and Stryker can reach it, a component in the right layer, a
mutation floor, and proof in a browser. Do it in that order.

You may work with a coding agent. The rules of the module still hold: the rung 5 gate stays green, the
fence stays where it is, and every line is yours to explain. Two changes are a person's to make, and for
this assignment you are that person: the new requirement, and the new line in `stryker.config.json`.
