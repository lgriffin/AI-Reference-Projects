# The brief

## The need

A student reading a course in order finishes a note and wants the next thing. Today they go back to the
topic page, find their place, and click again; at the end of a topic they go back twice. A pager removes
those clicks.

## What a student sees

On every talk, note and lab page, below the content, a navigation region labelled **Learning object
pager** with up to two links:

- `← <title>` to the previous learning object in reading order, unless this is the first;
- `<title> →` to the next one, unless this is the last.

**Reading order** is the order the course holds its talks, notes and labs in `tutors.json`: topic by
topic, unit by unit, side units where the course holds them. Courses, topics, units, sides, web links and
archives are not stops. For the sample course the order is **Welcome**, **Reading Guide**,
**Lab-01-First-Steps**.

## Specify first

Before any code, the rule goes into the variant the way R-014 to R-021 did, and the way you did it in
Labs 8 and 9:

1. the next id from `pnpm test:ears:audit --next-id`;
2. one EARS sentence in `REQUIREMENTS.md`, layer `reader`. It must say "the reader shall" and use the words
   "reading order", because that is the concept you are defining;
3. the same sentence as a `Rule:` in `tests/bdd/features/reader/reader.feature`, with scenarios for a stop
   in the middle, a stop at an end, and a page that is not a stop;
4. the steps bound, and the scenarios red.

Commit that red state before you write `lo-pager.ts`. Your history is part of the submission.

Use the method from the reading note "Specifying a user interface": what is **decided** (the order; the
neighbours; the ends) goes in `lib/course`; what is **displayed** (a `nav`, two links) goes in a component.

## What not to do

- Do not add a dependency or change `tests/rung-fence.test.ts`, `tests/architecture/` or the grading tests.
- Do not put the logic in the component: `lib/course` is plain TypeScript so that a scenario, Vitest and
  Stryker can reach it. `AGENTS.md` names the escape route and forbids it: "move a reader rule into a
  component to escape its scenario".
- Do not change the lab's own step pager. The two pagers sit one above the other and have different
  labels ("Step pager" and "Learning object pager").
- Do not lower a mutation floor. Add one for the new file; raise others if your tests earned it.
