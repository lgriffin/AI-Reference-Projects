# Rung 5 exit criteria

Rung 5 ends here. Before week 10, check each line. The first five are about understanding; the last three
are evidence you can show.

| You can | Level | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| explain every line of the summaries of `pnpm test` and `pnpm test:e2e` in rung 5, skipped tests included | Understand | Lab 8, step 2 |
| follow any reader URL from its route folder to the component that draws it, and to its 404 | Analyse | Lab 8, steps 5 and 6 |
| say, for each of R-014 to R-021, whether a scenario, a browser test or both prove it, and why | Evaluate | the reading note "Specifying a user interface"; Lab 9, step 6 |
| explain why a rune's `.value` is not the object you stored, and what that means for a `Map` keyed by objects | Analyse | Lab 9, steps 3 and 5 |
| break the Raw HTML rule and a layering rule, and read both messages | Analyse | Lab 9, step 4 |
| pass the grading tests (`pnpm test:assignment`: 6 passed, then 1 passed) with the gate green | Create | this assignment |
| show a mutation floor for `lo-pager.ts`, with every other floor held | Evaluate | this assignment, AC6 |
| compare your rung 5 with the standard version and name the requirement behind every difference | Evaluate | Lab 9, step 9 |

If a line is not yet true, it is the first thing to do in week 10. Rung 6 (Guarded) starts from rung 5's
standard version, so you lose nothing by climbing: your wall, your page titles and your pager stay in your
own copy, ready to carry forward when you choose.

Rung 6 will ask a harder question of the reader you now understand: what if the course's text is not
friendly? The one `{@html}` in `Markdown.svelte`, and the rule that keeps it the only one, are where that
week begins.
