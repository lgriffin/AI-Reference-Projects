# A next and previous navigator

Add a navigator to the rung 4 reader that lets a learner page through the course: from a talk, note or lab
to the previous and the next one, across units and topics, without going back to the topic page.

Upstream's lab has a step pager (`content/lab/Lab.svelte`), and its course navigation
(`packages/svelte/ui-navigators/src/CourseNavigation.svelte` with `LoContextTree.svelte`) shows a learner
where they are in the course. You will build a pager between learning objects, in the navigators layer, with
its logic tested, and prove it in a browser.

You may work with a coding agent. The rules of the course still hold: the rung 4 gate stays green, the
fence stays where it is, and every line is yours to explain.
