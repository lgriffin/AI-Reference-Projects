# Objectives

Follow one course page through the real code, from the URL to the rendered cards, using the mono-repo's own
guide: `docs/COURSE-PAGE-WALKTHROUGH.md`.

You will open every file the guide names, confirm it is there, and make one small visible change. At the end
you will hold a map of about a dozen files across four packages. The mono-repo's guide calls that the read
radius for the most common kind of change.

You need the reader running from Lab 1 (`pnpm dev`) and the mono-repo open in your editor. This course pins the
mono-repo at commit `2b96b69`; if you have pulled since, some lines may have moved.
