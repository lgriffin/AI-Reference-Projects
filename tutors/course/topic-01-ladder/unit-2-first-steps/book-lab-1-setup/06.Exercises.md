# Exercises

## Exercise 1: a talk

Add `topic-01-hello/talk-1-intro/` with a `talk.md` (first line the title, then a one-line summary) and a Marp
deck `intro.marp` that starts:

~~~markdown
---
marp: true
theme: default
paginate: true
---

# Hello, Tutors
~~~

Validate, regenerate and reload the reader. Then delete the `marp: true` line and validate again. Which rule
objects, and does its message tell you the fix?

## Exercise 2: a lab

Add `topic-01-hello/book-1-first-lab/` with two steps, `00.Lab-01-First.md` and `01.Second.md`, each starting
with a `#` heading. Add an image at `img/main.png`. What does the lab card show as its title? Look at
`buildLab` in `packages/jsr/gen/src/services/course-builder.ts` to find out why.

## Exercise 3: a second topic

Add `topic-03-later/` with a `topic.md`, skipping `topic-02`. Which warning appears, and at what severity? Why
is it a warning and not an error?

## Exercise 4: read the rules

Open `packages/jsr/course-rules/src/finding.ts` in the mono-repo. It lists every rule from V001 to V021 with
its severity. Pick three you have not triggered yet and break your course to trigger each.
