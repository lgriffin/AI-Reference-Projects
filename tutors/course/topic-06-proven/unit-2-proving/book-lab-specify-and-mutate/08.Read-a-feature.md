# Read a feature file

Open `tests/bdd/features/generator/course-json.feature` and its steps,
`tests/bdd/steps/generator/course-json.steps.ts`, in your clone of tutors-mono-repo. Answer each question
in a sentence or two.

## The Rules

1. The feature has a `Background:` with one Given. What does it produce, and why is it a Background and
   not a Given in each scenario?
2. Find `@rule-0265`. Compare its sentence with rung 5's `R-004`. What does upstream's say that rung 5's
   does not, and the other way round?
3. Find `@rule-0266`. It is the assignment in this topic. Which file does the course carry the ignore list
   in, and what happens to an ignored topic?
4. How many Rules does the feature have? Which EARS patterns do they use?

## The steps

5. Find `describeFeature(feature, ({ Background, Rule }) => {`. Where does `json` come from?
6. Find the Rule that refuses "a field the schema does not name". Which function's output does its Then
   compare, and where does that function live?
7. Find a step that takes `{string}` and splits it with `", "`. Rung 5's `list` helper does the same. Why
   are lists passed as one quoted string rather than as a Gherkin table?

## The other side

8. Open `.claude/skills/ears-gherkin-dev/SKILL.md`. Its step 2 ends with "wait for a yes". Who says yes,
   and to what? Which of rung 5's `AGENTS.md` sections says the same?
9. Upstream's tags are `@rule-NNNN`; rung 5's are `@R-NNN` with a table in `REQUIREMENTS.md`. Give one
   advantage of each.
