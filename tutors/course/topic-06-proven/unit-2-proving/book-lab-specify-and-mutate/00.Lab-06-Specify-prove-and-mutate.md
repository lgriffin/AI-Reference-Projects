# Objectives

Take one new rule through every tier of rung 5, then run and read the same machinery upstream.

You will write an EARS requirement and watch the closure check refuse it, write a Gherkin scenario and
watch it fail, make it pass, then run Stryker and find that every mutant in your new code survives. You
will find out why, and kill them. Finally you will run a slice of tutors-mono-repo's own tests and read one
of its feature files.

By the end of this lab you will have:

- a requirement `R-013` in `REQUIREMENTS.md`, proved by a scenario and implemented in `packages/lite`
- a unit test that kills the mutants the scenario could not, and every mutation floor held
- run upstream's EARS audit, its audit's own tests and a generator feature's steps

You need the variant at `tutors/variants/rung-5-proven` in your clone of AI-Reference-Projects, and your
clone of tutors-mono-repo from Orientation Lab 1. Allow about ninety minutes.
