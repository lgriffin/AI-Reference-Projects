# Acceptance criteria

Written as EARS sentences, one "shall" each, as rung 5 taught.

| Id | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| AC1 | The composed variant shall hold `README.md`, `RUNG.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `package.json` and `tests/rung-fence.test.ts`, as every variant does. |
| AC2 | When `pnpm test` is run in `variants/summit-composed`, the composed variant shall exit with status 0. |
| AC3 | The composed variant shall keep every test file of rungs 4, 5, 6 and 7, at the same path under `tests/`, except each rung's `rung-fence.test.ts`, which is merged into one. |
| AC4 | The composed fence shall allow the union of the dependencies and capabilities that rungs 4 to 7 allow, and shall reject anything else, naming the reason in its failure message. |
| AC5 | `RUNG.md` shall name each of the four composed rungs, the capability each brings, and an upstream mirror table that covers `apps/reader`, `tests/`, `packages/jsr/identity` and `.github/workflows`. |
| AC6 | `AGENTS.md` shall be under 500 words and shall have a "Rung Fence" section and an "Ask a human first" section. |
| AC7 | `RUNG.md` shall list every conflict met while composing, with how it was resolved and the upstream file that informed the resolution. |
| AC8 | If a test of one rung had to change for the composition, then `RUNG.md` shall name the test, the change and the reason. |
| AC9 | The history shall show each rung's fence widening as its own commit, made by a person. |

AC1, AC3, AC5 and AC6 are checked by the grading test in the next step. AC2 is the variant's own gate. AC4 is
checked by your fence test, which you must show failing on a dependency none of rungs 4 to 7 allows. AC7 to AC9
are read by a person.
