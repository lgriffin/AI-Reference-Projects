# Compose the ladder

A capstone. Rungs 4 to 7 each branch from rung 3: a Svelte reader, a proven test suite, a guarded build and a
shipped release, each on its own. In tutors-mono-repo they live together. Your task is to compose them into
one variant, `variants/summit-composed`, that has all four capabilities, one gate and one fence.

This is the step that turns four branches back into one line of history. When the ladder moves to its own
repository, your composition is the merge of the `rung-4-reader` to `rung-7-shipped` branches.

You may work with a coding agent. The rules of the course still hold: the fence is yours to write, and every
line is yours to explain.
