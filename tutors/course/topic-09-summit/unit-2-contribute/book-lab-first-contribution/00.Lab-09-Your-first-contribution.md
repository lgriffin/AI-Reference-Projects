# Objectives

Make one real contribution to tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo with a coding agent, under the ladder.

You will pick a good first issue, find the rung it sits on, fence your agent with a session manifest, run the
repository's own gates, and open a pull request a maintainer can review in one sitting.

You need the mono-repo cloned and running from Orientation Lab 1, and a GitHub account. For the full test
suite you also need `kubectl` or `kustomize` on your `PATH`.

The work is yours. The agent helps; the fence keeps its help at your level; the gates and the reviewer decide.
