# Exercises

## Exercise 1: the fence held?

Paste the output of `git diff --stat` for your PR beside your session manifest's "May touch" list. Did every
path match? If not, what did you change, and why?

## Exercise 2: levels met

List each check that turned red while you worked. For each, give its Assertion Ladder level (L3 or L4), and
say whether its message told you the fix.

## Exercise 3: a missing level

Name one thing the reviewer, or you, had to know that no check or document stated. Write the sentence that
would put it at L2, and say where in the repository it belongs.
