# Pick a good first issue

Open the [`good first issue`](https://github.com/tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo/labels/good%20first%20issue)
list. CONTRIBUTING.md promises that each one "names the files to change, the expected result and how to check
it. Most touch one package and need no knowledge of the wider architecture."

Read three or four. For each, write down:

- the files it names
- the expected result
- how to check it

Choose one you could explain to a friend in two sentences. If nothing fits, the documentation drift you found
in Orientation Lab 2 (section 2 of `docs/COURSE-PAGE-WALKTHROUGH.md` names a `+layout.server.ts` that is now
`+layout.ts`) is a fair docs-only contribution. Search the open issues and pull requests first, in case
someone has already reported or fixed it.

## Claim it

Comment "I'll take this" on the issue. For a good first issue that is enough. For anything larger,
CONTRIBUTING.md asks you to discuss your approach on the issue first.
