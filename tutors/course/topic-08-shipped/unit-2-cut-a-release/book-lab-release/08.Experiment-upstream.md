# Experiment on upstream

Read, do not change. Everything here is in `$MONO` (tutors-mono-repo at `2b96b69`) and a clone of the
release harness:

~~~bash
git clone https://github.com/tutors-sdk/tutors-release-harness.git ~/src/tutors-release-harness
export HARNESS=~/src/tutors-release-harness
~~~

## 1. The release SOP

Open `$MONO/release/SOP.md`. For each of your eight steps, find the upstream step it corresponds to, and
write down the upstream steps you had no counterpart for. Which of them exist because Tutors has a
production to compare with (an A/A, rehearsals, a watch window)?

Find the "Stop the line" rules. Which one forbids re-running for a better number, and what is a re-run
allowed to do?

## 2. A claim there and here

Open `$MONO/release/claims.yaml` and `$MONO/release/README.md`, "Writing a claim". Pick one claim and say,
in one sentence, what difference from production it waives. Then compare with one of rung 7's claims: what
does each kind of claim make the release *do* when it is wrong?

Run upstream's check if you have its dependencies installed:

~~~bash
cd $MONO && pnpm install && pnpm check:release-claims
~~~

## 3. The candidate ships

In `$MONO/.github/workflows/image-build.yml`, read the header comment. Then open
`$MONO/scripts/promote-image.ts` and find the four checks that must hold before a candidate is promoted.
Which one would have caught a rebuild with a fresh `apt-get upgrade`?

## 4. What is production?

Compare `$MONO/release/deployed.json` with `$MONO/deploy/k8s/overlays/reader/kustomization.yaml`. Do the
reader digests agree? Which check fails when they do not (`$MONO/scripts/checks/deploy-pins.ts`)?

## 5. The harness's A3 and scoreboard

Open the harness's published pages: the A3 at `https://tutors-sdk.github.io/tutors-release-harness/a3.html`,
the readiness page and the scoreboard, all linked from its README. Find the Gate, the band and the
countermeasures on the A3.

In the clone, `$HARNESS/scoreboard/releases.jsonl` is empty at 1.63.0: the lines are written by CI runs and
published with the pages. Read `$HARNESS/src/scoreboard/line.ts`. Why is the scoreboard append-only, and
what does a re-run of the same tag add?

## 6. Lean, as the harness writes it

Read `$HARNESS/docs/lean.md`, "The five ideas", and `$HARNESS/kaizen/README.md`, "What opens a 5 Whys".
Your readiness A3 has a root cause section. Which of the seven countermeasure kinds the harness allows
would you choose for a claim that kept losing its evidence?
