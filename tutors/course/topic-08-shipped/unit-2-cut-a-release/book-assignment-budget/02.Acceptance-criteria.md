# Acceptance criteria

Written as EARS sentences, one "shall" each.

| Id | Criterion |
| --- | --- |
| AC1 | The SLO check shall know the measure `largest-page-bytes`, in bytes, whose value is the size of the largest file ending in `.html`. |
| AC2 | When the largest page is over its budget, the SLO check shall fail with a message that names the objective, the largest page's path, by how much it is over, and that a budget is never raised to make a release pass. |
| AC3 | `release/slo.yaml` shall hold an objective with `measure: largest-page-bytes` and a budget of at most 16384 bytes, and the fixture site shall be within it. |
| AC4 | `release/claims.yaml` shall hold a claim `pages-within-budget` whose evidence is a test that exists, runs and exercises `largest-page-bytes`. |
| AC5 | While the assignment is in progress, `pnpm test` shall stay green, and `pnpm release:claims` and `pnpm release:slo` shall pass. |
| AC6 | If a later change makes a page larger than the budget, then `pnpm release:slo` shall exit with status 1. |

AC1 to AC4 are checked by the grading test. AC5 is the variant's own gate and checks. AC6 you show in your
pull request: lower the budget below the largest page, paste the failure, put it back.
