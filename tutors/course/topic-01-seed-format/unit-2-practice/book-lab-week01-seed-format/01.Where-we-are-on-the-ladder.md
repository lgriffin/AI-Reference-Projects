## What this week adds

Nothing has been built yet, so this week adds the foundations every later week stands on:

- the **course format**: the grammar of folder names and files that every Tutors tool reads;
- the **validator**: the upstream rules, run by one command, that say whether a folder is a compliant course;
- the **first CLI**: rung 1's `node src/main.ts <courseFolder> [outFile]`, which writes `tutors.json`.

Next week stays on rung 1 and goes inside it: the types that make a forgotten kind a compile error, and the
test that holds rung 1 to what upstream writes.
