## Checklist

- [ ] `pnpm test:assignment` shows `5 passed`

- [ ] `pnpm release:claims` reports 15 claims, and `pnpm release:slo` reports four objectives within budget
- [ ] the requirement and its scenario were committed, and approved by a person, before the code
- [ ] every commit since `v1.0.0` is a Conventional Commit (`pnpm release:version --from v1.0.0` warns only
      about the lab's "Update notes")
- [ ] the release that holds your work is tagged, and `CHANGELOG.md` says what a learner gets, in one line
      you curated
- [ ] `pnpm check:mutation-floors` passes after a fresh `pnpm test:mutation`, with no floor lowered
- [ ] the Rung Fence, `tests/architecture/`, the `Dockerfile`, the workflow and the other budgets are unchanged
- [ ] in your pull request: the failure from AC7, pasted; one sentence on why you chose your budget; your
      "before" and "after" value stream maps, with one sentence on what the new objective cost in process
      time and whether the promise is worth it

- [ ] your pull request says where you worked with a coding agent, and that you can explain every line
