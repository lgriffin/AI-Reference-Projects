# The grading test

It is already in the variant: `tests/assignment/shipped.grading.test.ts`, labelled "Assignment 7". Read it
before you write any code; it is part of the specification. Three things to notice:

- **It is skipped by default.** Each `describe` is skipped unless `LADDER_ASSIGNMENT=1`, so the variant's
  gate stays green until you start (the gate's `5 skipped`). `pnpm test:assignment` sets the variable.
- **It finds your requirement by its words, not its id.** AC1 looks after R-044 for a sentence with
  "largest page" and "the release shall", then runs the whole closure audit. So the lab's R-045 does not
  get in its way.
- **It is not evidence.** A claim that cites a file under `tests/assignment` fails the Closure Check: a
  grading test never runs in the gate. Your claim needs a test of your own.

## Grade it

~~~bash
pnpm test:assignment
~~~

Before you start:

~~~
     × AC1: REQUIREMENTS.md holds a release requirement about the largest page, proved by a Rule
     × AC2: largest-page-bytes measures the largest HTML page, not the largest file
     × AC3: a page over budget fails with a message that names the page and teaches
     × AC4: release/slo.yaml has a largest-page objective, and the real site is within it
     × AC5: release/claims.yaml promises it, resting on your requirement, with evidence that exists and runs
...
      Tests  5 failed (5)
~~~

When you are done:

~~~
 Test Files  1 passed (1)
      Tests  5 passed (5)
~~~

Every failure names its criterion and the fix. For example:

~~~
AC1: add a requirement after R-044 (pnpm test:ears:audit --next-id) that says what the release shall do when the largest page of the site is over its budget. Name "the largest page" and "the release" in it.
AC2: add "largest-page-bytes" to MEASURES in scripts/lib/slo.ts
AC4: add an objective with measure: largest-page-bytes to release/slo.yaml, with a why
~~~

## What it checks

| Test | How |
| --- | --- |
| AC1 | a requirement after R-044 names "largest page" and "the release shall", and `pnpm test:ears:audit` has no finding |
| AC2 | `MEASURES["largest-page-bytes"]` exists, its unit is `bytes`, it reads 5200 from the hand-built measurement and 0 from a build with no page |
| AC3 | `evaluate` with a 4096-byte budget fails, and the message contains `SLO "largest-page" exceeded`, the page's path and "Never raise a budget to make a release pass" |
| AC4 | `release/slo.yaml` is valid, has your objective with a budget of 4096 or less and a `why` of more than twenty characters, and a real build of the fixture is within it |
| AC5 | the claim `pages-within-budget` passes the Closure Check on its own, lists your requirement, cites a test outside `tests/assignment` whose file mentions `largest-page-bytes`, and the whole claims file is clean |

Then run the rest yourself:

~~~bash
pnpm test
pnpm test:ears:audit
pnpm release:claims
pnpm release:slo
~~~

~~~
release/claims.yaml: 15 claims, each with evidence that exists and runs.
...
ok   largest-page: the size of the largest page is 1.9 kB, within its budget of 4.0 kB (51% headroom).
~~~

Your id, budget and headroom may differ. Do not change the expectations to make the test pass: change the
code.
