# Acceptance criteria

Written as EARS sentences, one "shall" each. Each maps to a module outcome in `course.md`. AC1 to AC6 are
assessed at **Create**, because together they are one promise you specify, build and prove. AC7 and AC8
are assessed at **Evaluate**.

| Id | Criterion | Module outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AC1 | `REQUIREMENTS.md` shall hold, after R-044, a requirement that names the largest page and says what the release shall do when it is over its budget, and the EARS audit shall report no finding. | M6 |
| AC2 | The SLO check shall know the measure `largest-page-bytes`, in bytes, whose value is the size of the largest file ending in `.html`, and 0 for a build with no page. | M8 |
| AC3 | If the largest page is over its budget, then the SLO check shall fail with a message that names the objective, the largest page's path, by how much it is over, and that a budget is never raised to make a release pass. | M8, M7 |
| AC4 | `release/slo.yaml` shall hold an objective with `measure: largest-page-bytes`, a budget of at most 4096 bytes and a `why`, and the fixture site shall be within it. | M7 |
| AC5 | `release/claims.yaml` shall hold a claim `pages-within-budget` that rests on your requirement and whose evidence is a test of yours, outside `tests/assignment`, that exists, runs and exercises `largest-page-bytes`. | M7, M8 |
| AC6 | When `pnpm test` is run, the variant shall pass, and `pnpm test:ears:audit`, `pnpm release:claims` and `pnpm release:slo` shall pass, with the Rung Fence, the architecture tests, the other budgets and the other claims unchanged. | M8, M2 |
| AC7 | If a later change makes a page larger than the budget, then `pnpm release:slo` shall exit with status 1. | M7 |
| AC8 | The history shall show the requirement and its scenario committed before the code that implements them, in Conventional Commits. | M6 |

AC1 to AC5 are checked by the grading test in the next step. AC6 is the variant's own gate and checks.
AC7 you show in your submission: lower the budget below the largest page, paste the failure, put it back.
AC8 is read by a person.

## The cases the grading test uses

One measurement, built by hand: `index.html` at 3000 bytes, a lab page
(`topic-02-practice/unit-1-lab/book-1-first-lab/Setup.html`) at 5200 bytes, `tutors.json` at 9000 bytes and
`version.json` at 120 bytes. The largest *file* is `tutors.json`; the largest *page* is the lab page. A
second measurement has no page at all. For AC4 the test builds the real fixture, whose largest page is
about 2 kB.
