# Assignment 7: every page within budget

Rung 7's coding assignment (A7 in `course.md`), assessed at the **Create** level of Bloom's taxonomy, with
a judgement at **Evaluate**. It assesses module outcomes M7 (evaluate the release readiness of a system from evidence:
release claims, budgets) and M8 (contribute an improvement under the ladder, using standard work and
Kaizen: practised here on rung 7, with its release SOP and a measured value stream, before you do it
upstream in week 12).

Rung 7 keeps a budget for the whole site. But a learner never downloads the whole site. They open one
page, on a phone, on a bus. Your task is to make the release promise something about that page, and to
build everything the promise needs:

- a **requirement** that says what the release shall do when the largest page is over its budget;
- a **scenario** that proves the sentence, seen red;
- a **measure**, `largest-page-bytes`, with advice that names the page;
- an **objective** in `release/slo.yaml`, with a budget chosen from a measurement and a `why`;
- a **test** of your own, and a **claim**, `pages-within-budget`, that rests on your requirement and cites
  your test.

This is the week in miniature: a sentence, a number written down, a check that holds it, and a promise
that cannot be made without evidence. The code is about fifteen lines. The chain is the work.

You may work with a coding agent. The Rung Fence and the architecture rules still hold, the requirement is
yours to write and have approved by a person, and every line is yours to explain.
