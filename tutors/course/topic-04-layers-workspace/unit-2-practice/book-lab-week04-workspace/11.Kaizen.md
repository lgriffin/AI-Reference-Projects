# Kaizen (Evaluate)

Kaizen means small, continuous improvement. Spend ten minutes on it at the end of every lab.

## Name one waste

Pick one waste you met this week, in the variant or in your own way of working. Candidates from this lab:

- **Handoffs and coupling.** Which arrow in rung 3 would cause the most handoffs if it were reversed?
- **Delay.** How long did `pnpm test` take in step 2? How much of that is `tsc` run five times? Try
  `time pnpm typecheck` and compare.
- **Relearning.** Which failure message this week did you have to read twice? What was missing from it?

## Find its root cause

Ask "why?" until you reach something you could change. For example: "I read the Closure message twice" →
"it gave a fix that was wrong for my case" → "it does not know the layer's reason" → "the Closure rule runs
without looking at `LAYERS`".

## Propose one improvement

Write one change to the standard work (the gate, `AGENTS.md`, a rule's message, or your own routine) that
would remove the waste. Make it if it is small and inside your rung; otherwise, write it down as a proposal.
Note it in your learning log: you will look back at your Kaizen notes in week 12.
