## What this week adds

Rung 3 is a copy of rung 2 with nothing new for the user and everything new for the next contributor:

- a **workspace** of four packages (`model`, `gen`, `lite`, `cli`) linked with `workspace:*`;
- **dependency direction**: every arrow points inward to `model`, kept by an architecture test;
- the **compiler in the gate**: `pnpm test` runs `tsc` for every package before any test.

Next week stays on rung 3 and goes to its edges: the port that keeps `node:fs` out of `gen`, its two
adapters, and the contract that keeps the test double honest.

## The value it delivers

Rung 3's customer is the **contributor**. The value is a change that stays in one package: a new page layout
touches `lite` only, a new kind of folder touches `gen` and `model` only, and nobody else waits.
