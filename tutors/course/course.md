# Tutors Ladder

Rebuild Tutors, the open learning toolkit, from its simplest working version up to the full
[tutors-mono-repo](https://github.com/tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo). This course is for people who would like to
contribute to Tutors and want to understand it by building it.

The course climbs two ladders at once.

| Ladder | What it is | Where you meet it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The **capability ladder** | Seven runnable versions of Tutors. Each needs a little more expertise than the one below and delivers a major new capability: a generator, a static site, a layered workspace, a Svelte reader, a proven test suite, a guarded build and a shipped release. | One topic per rung, then the Summit: the real repository. |
| The **Assertion Ladder** | Six levels at which a rule can be kept: L0 Implied, L1 Shown, L2 Told, L3 Checked, L4 Explained, L5 Prevented. | Every rung carries a short manifest and a **Rung Fence**, a test that stops a coding agent from building beyond the rung you are on. |

You are welcome to work with a coding agent. The fence keeps its help at your level. Climbing a rung means
lifting the fence on purpose, once you understand what the next rung adds.

| Topic | Rung | Track |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation | - | Tutors, the two ladders, setup, your first course |
| Seed | 1 | CLI: a course folder in, `tutors.json` out |
| Lite | 2 | UI (static): a static HTML site |
| Layers | 3 | Architecture: a layered pnpm workspace with ports |
| Reader | 4 | UI (app): an interactive Svelte 5 reader |
| Proven | 5 | Testing: tests as specification |
| Guarded | 6 | Security: safe content, private courses, a secure supply chain |
| Shipped | 7 | Releasing: a versioned, gated, containerised release |
| Summit | all | Contributing to tutors-mono-repo with an agent, under the ladder |
