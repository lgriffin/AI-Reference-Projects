## What climbing means

Rung 3 keeps rung 2's behaviour and changes its structure: the same tools as a pnpm workspace of packages,
with ports between them and an architecture test that keeps dependencies pointing one way. Its fence allows
TypeScript as a dev dependency and `@ladder/*` workspace imports, and its gate gains `tsc --noEmit`, so the
L5 checks you ran by hand on rungs 1 and 2 become part of the gate. Week 4 starts from rung 2's standard
version. Read `variants/rung-3-layers/RUNG.md` before you start.
