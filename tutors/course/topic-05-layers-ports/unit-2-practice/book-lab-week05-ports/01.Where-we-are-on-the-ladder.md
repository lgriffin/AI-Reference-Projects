## What this week adds

Last week you kept the arrows pointing inward. This week is about the edges:

- a **port**, `FileSystemPort`, owned by `gen`, and two **adapters**, `MemoryFileSystem` and `NodeFileSystem`;
- the **composition root**, `apps/cli/src/run.ts`, the one place that knows every piece;
- the **Honest Double**: one contract run against both adapters, and a rule that every adapter runs it;
- the **Port rule**, with the compiler as a second wall.

This is rung 3's last week: it ends with the assignment, the exit criteria in the topic page, and a
comparison with rung 3's standard version.
