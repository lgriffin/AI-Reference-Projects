# Five Whys on a red build (Analyse, Evaluate)

Lean's root cause tool is simple: ask "why?" until the answer is something in the **process** you can
change. Never stop at a person ("I forgot"); ask why the system let you forget.

## A worked example

The red build from step 5, before you added the adapter to `ADAPTERS`: `not ok 40`, the Honest Double rule.

1. **Why is it red?** `ZipFileSystem` implements the port but is not in `ADAPTERS`.
2. **Why is it not there?** The author did not know the contract existed.
3. **Why not?** `AGENTS.md` says it, but the author started from `file-system-port.ts`, as most people would.
4. **Why does that file not say so?** Its header names the adapters but not the contract.
5. **Why does that matter?** The rule is told in one place and checked in another, and the place people look
   says neither.

**Countermeasure:** one sentence in the header of `packages/gen/src/file-system-port.ts`: "Every adapter
joins ADAPTERS in apps/cli/tests/file-system-port.contract.test.ts (the Honest Double rule)."
