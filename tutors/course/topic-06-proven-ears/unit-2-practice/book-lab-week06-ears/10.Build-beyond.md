# Build beyond (Create)

Pick two. Each starts from the value it adds. Changes to `scripts/checks/` or `tests/suite-health/` are
yours to make, not your agent's, and need negative fixtures in `tests/suite-health/requirements.test.ts`
first.

1. **`dual-scenarios` for rung 4.** Value: the first state-driven or optional requirement in rung 4 (rung 5's
   `--json-only`) cannot be proved by one side alone. Add upstream's check to `auditFeatures`: a Rule whose
   requirement is State-driven or Optional needs a scenario tagged `@active` and one tagged `@inactive`.
   Prove it can fail on a fixture before you run it on the repository.
2. **A pattern that matches its words.** Value: a contributor who writes "When" under "Ubiquitous" learns at
   once. Upstream's `ears-tag-mismatch` derives the pattern from the opening word. Rung 4 checks the opening
   against the declared pattern; add the reverse check, and decide what a complex sentence should do.
3. **A requirements page.** Value: a reviewer reads the requirements with their proof in one place. Write a
   script that prints, for each requirement, its sentence, its scenarios and the tests that cite it, as
   Markdown. Upstream's `scripts/ears-dashboard.ts` is the big version.
4. **Your own requirement, proved.** Value: a rule you care about becomes part of the product. Take the best
   row from step 6 and take it through next week's loop now: a Rule, a scenario seen red, the code, green.
