## Find one Rule of each pattern

~~~bash
cd $MONO/tests/bdd/features
grep -rn -A1 "@ears-state-driven" . | grep "Rule:" | head -3
grep -rn -A1 "@ears-optional" . | grep "Rule:" | head -3
~~~

1. Pick one state-driven and one optional Rule. Find its `@active` and `@inactive` scenarios. What does the
   `@inactive` one prove that the `@active` one cannot?
2. Find `@rule-0050` in `ui/learning-activities.feature`. It is a complex requirement. Which clause decided
   its tag?
3. Open `generator/course-json.feature` and find `@rule-0265`. Compare it with rung 4's `R-004`. What does
   each say that the other does not?
4. Open `$MONO/guides/testing/EARS-METHODOLOGY.md`, "The EARS audit". Which of upstream's codes does rung
   4's audit not have? Pick one and say what it would catch in rung 4.
