# Objectives

Start rung 4 from rung 3's standard version. Read its requirements and classify them, break the closure
that ties them to their scenarios, critique sentences with the audit and with your own judgement, then
formalise the informal rules you wrote in weeks 1 to 5. Compare rung 4's standard work for requirements with
upstream's, run upstream's audit, build beyond, and end with a Kaizen step.

## Learning outcomes

| Outcome | Level | Module | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explain the five EARS patterns and the complex form, with an example of each | Understand | M6 | 3, 7 |
| Write a requirement with an id, a pattern, a system and a layer, and pass it through the audit | Apply | M6 | 6, 7 |
| Trace a requirement to its Rule, steps and code, and diagnose each closure finding | Analyse | M4, M6 | 3, 4 |
| Critique a requirement for the audit's faults and for the faults only a person sees | Evaluate | M6 | 5, 6 |
| Formalise your "Name the rule" sentences from weeks 1 to 5 | Create | M6 | 6 |
| Judge rung 4's standard work for requirements against upstream's, and improve it | Evaluate | M6, M8 | 8, 11 |

Each step's heading names its Bloom level. Bring your "Name the rule" sentences from weeks 1 to 5. Allow
about two hours.
