# Kaizen (Evaluate)

## Name one waste

Candidates from this week:

- **Relearning.** A sentence from weeks 1 to 5 that you had to rewrite from scratch. What did the first
  version lack?
- **Rework.** A sentence you would have argued about after the code, had nobody read it first.
- **Defects.** A fault from step 5's second list that no check would have caught.
- **Waiting.** How long did the audit take, compared with the whole gate?

## Find its root cause

Ask "why?" until you reach the standard work: the audit, `AGENTS.md`, `REQUIREMENTS.md`'s guidance, or your
own habit.

## Improve the standard work

Make or propose one change. Your step 8 proposal counts, if it removes the waste you named. Record it in your
learning log.
