# Acceptance criteria

Written as EARS sentences: one "shall" each. Each is mapped to the week's learning outcomes (topic
descriptor, outcomes 1 to 5) and to the module outcomes in `course.md` (M1 to M8). There is no grading test
this time: the repository's own gates and your evidence stand in for it.

| Id | Criterion | Week outcome | Module outcome | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AC1 | The submission shall name the improvement, the person who benefits from it, and the issue on which it was claimed after a search for existing reports. | 3 | M8 | Evaluate |
| AC2 | The submission shall name the rung the change sits on and the upstream paths that rung mirrors, and shall explain what the changed code does in the path from course folder to reader. | 1 | M1, M4 | Analyse |
| AC3 | The requirement shall be an EARS Rule with a system name, exactly one "shall", an id and a pattern tag, with at least one Given/When/Then scenario whose Then is observable. | 4 | M6 | Create |
| AC4 | When the Rule is checked with `pnpm test:ears:audit` (with `--strict` for a scratch Rule), the audit shall report no violation other than `unbound-feature` on a scratch Rule. | 4 | M6 | Evaluate |
| AC5 | Where the change alters observable behaviour, the submission shall show the scenario failing before the implementation and passing after it. | 4, 5 | M3, M6 | Create |
| AC6 | The session manifest shall be under 500 words and shall hold the sections Task, Rung, May touch, Must not touch, Read first, Gate and Ask a human first. | 4 | M2 | Create |
| AC7 | The pull request's `git diff --stat` shall list only paths on the manifest's "May touch" list, or the A3 shall explain each exception. | 4, 5 | M2 | Evaluate |
| AC8 | When the repository's gates are run on the branch, they shall pass, with no test, baseline, floor or fence loosened; a baseline line the fix makes obsolete shall be deleted. | 5 | M2, M3, M4 | Apply |
| AC9 | The A3 shall fit one page and shall hold the sections Value, Current state, Root cause, Countermeasure and Evidence. | 3, 5 | M8 | Evaluate |
| AC10 | The A3's root cause shall be reached by asking why at least three times and shall not end at a person. | 3 | M4, M8 | Analyse |
| AC11 | The A3 shall state at which Assertion Ladder level the rule was kept before the change and at which level it is kept after it, citing the file, and shall judge whether that level fits the risk. | 2 | M5, M7 | Evaluate |
| AC12 | The pull request shall use upstream's template, tick only the checks that were run, and state that a coding agent was used. | 5 | M2, M8 | Apply |

"The repository's gates" are `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm check` upstream (a docs-only change may run
the relevant tier instead and say so), or `pnpm test` and the release checks on rung 7.

AC5 applies only to a behaviour change. For a change that restores an existing Rule, show instead the
command that demonstrated the problem before the change and its output after.
