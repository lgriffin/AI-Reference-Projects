## Write it in EARS

Decide, using last week's reading note:

- **Pattern.** It happens when a lab has more than one step: event-driven, "When ..., the ... shall ...".
- **System.** It is about a page: the site.
- **Layer.** The site lives in lite.
- **Observable response.** The text "Step 2 of 3" on the page.

Add this row under `R-012` in `REQUIREMENTS.md`:

~~~markdown
| R-013 | Event-driven | When a lab has more than one step, the site shall label each step page with its position, such as Step 2 of 3. | lite |
~~~
